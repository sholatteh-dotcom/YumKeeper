## Full Description (4,000 characters max)

**Meet YumKeeper — your kitchen's best friend for fresher food, happier meals, and a little more money in your pocket.**

The average household wastes £800 worth of food every year — most of it because we simply forget what's in the fridge. YumKeeper fixes that. It's the smart pantry manager that tracks every item in your fridge, freezer, and cupboards, alerts you before food expires, and tells you exactly how to keep it fresh for longer.

**Track everything in one place**
Add food items in seconds by scanning a barcode or typing a name. YumKeeper stores the expiry date, storage location (fridge, freezer, or pantry), and quantity for every item. Your entire household inventory is always one tap away — no more opening the fridge three times before shopping.

**Never miss an expiry date**
YumKeeper sends smart push notifications before your food expires — not after. Choose your alert window (1, 2, or 3 days before expiry) and get a morning digest every Sunday showing everything that needs using up that week. No more discovering furry leftovers at the back of the shelf.

**Expert preservation tips, instantly**
Not sure how long cooked chicken lasts in the fridge? Or whether you can freeze brie? YumKeeper's built-in preservation guide covers hundreds of foods with expert storage advice, freezing tips, and shelf-life guidance — all available offline, no internet required.

**Build smarter shopping lists**
When you mark an item as used or expired, YumKeeper can automatically add it to your shopping list. Share the list with your household in real time, tick items off as you shop, and never buy a duplicate again.

**Save money, reduce waste**
YumKeeper users report saving an average of £60 per month by reducing food waste. Over a year, that's a family holiday. The app pays for itself in the first week.

**Designed for every household**
Whether you're a meal-prepper, a busy parent, or just someone who hates throwing food away, YumKeeper works for you. Simple enough to use in 30 seconds, powerful enough to manage a full household pantry.

**Privacy first**
YumKeeper works fully offline. Your food data stays on your device and is never sold to third parties. Full GDPR compliance, available in English, French, German, Spanish, and Dutch.
